# Art credits

Sprites under `assets/oga/` are the classic game's weapons, impacts, and XP gems. Each one is [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). The hunter sheet and the graveyard tileset are PixelLab art and are noted in the [README](../README.md).

Only the frames the game draws are stored here. The packed files are about 5 KB.

## Pixel Art Spells

- **Used for:** the stake (`light_bolt.png`, the Light Bolt strip), the warding censer (`magic_orb.png`), the pyre flask (`fireball.png`), and the pyre pool (`firebomb.png`)
- **Author:** DevWizard
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-art-spells
- **License:** CC0

DevWizard asks to be credited as DevWizard when credit is given. CC0 does not require it.
